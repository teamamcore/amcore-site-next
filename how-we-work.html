Book a Discovery Call

In person, at your shop.

With the people who do the work. No fixed dates. We move at the pace of your jobs, and you get a real timeline on the discovery call.

The stages.

  1. Discovery with the owner

    What you want the business to look like, what keeps you up, and what you need to see.

  2. Discovery with the field

    Time with the crews. How a job really runs, what gets written down, what gets lost.

  3. Discovery with the office

    Time with whoever does the scheduling, the billing, and the chasing.

  4. Configuration

    We build it around your forms, your terms, and your process.

  5. A trial on real jobs

    A few crews run it on live work. We fix what they find.

  6. Training at the shop, then rollout

    We train your people in person and turn it on for everyone.

  7. A person who keeps it running

    Someone you can call. Updates and security stay with us.

What we need from you.

Time with the people who actually do the work, office and field.

Copies of the paperwork you use today. Work orders, estimates, invoices.

A few crews willing to run it on real jobs before the full rollout.
